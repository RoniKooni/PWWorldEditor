Editor Help

Controls:

- Middle mouse drag pans the canvas.

- Mouse wheel zooms the canvas.

- Left click places blocks with the selected hotbar slot.

- Right click erases editable blocks.

- Arrange selects and moves whole layers. Shift-click or drag a box to select multiple layers.

- Select creates a box selection when you hold and drag.

Hotkeys:

- Default: M Move, A Arrange, Q Select, F Fill, S Shapes, K Pick.

- 0-9 selects hotbar slots.

- Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo.

- Open View > Settings to change grid color, animated blocks, and keybinds.
